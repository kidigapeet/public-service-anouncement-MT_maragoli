Model Training & Benchmark Details

Research specifications and evaluation breakdown

Language Extension: Maragoli (Lulogooli, ISO 639-3: rag) was introduced into NLLB vocabulary using special token rag_Latn. The embedding weights were seeded from Kikuyu (kik_Latn) with 1% Gaussian noise.

Dataset Splits: 4,184 total parallel pairs (3,558 Train, 313 Dev, 313 Test) split in an 85 / 7.5 / 7.5 ratio.

Generation Constraints: Tested beam search with no_repeat_ngram_size=3 and repetition_penalty=1.2 to eliminate cyclic Bantu prefix stuttering.

Academic Context: Data Science Project, USIU-Africa, 2026.
